# Yogi Reader 📖

<img src="icon_preview.png" width="120" align="right" />

**Read every kind of book and document, and highlight and take notes like on a Kindle.** No ads, no account. Books are read offline; the dictionary uses the internet only when you look up a word.

## Reads
| | |
|---|---|
| **PDF** | Mozilla's pdf.js. Text can be selected, highlighted and searched. Pinch to zoom, outline, go to page. Dark and sepia pages. |
| **EPUB** | Chapters, table of contents and pictures |
| **Kindle** (MOBI, AZW, AZW3, PRC) | Text and pictures. DRM-locked books can't be opened. |
| **Word** (DOCX, ODT) | Headings (in the contents), bold, italic, lists, tables and pictures |
| **Excel** (XLSX) | Every sheet as a table |
| **PowerPoint** (PPTX) | Each slide's text and pictures |
| **More** | FB2, Markdown, CSV, HTML and plain text |

## Like a Kindle
* **Select words → Highlight, Note, Define, Translate, Copy or Share.**
* **Highlights** come in 4 colours. Notes appear as dotted underlines; tap one to edit, recolour or delete it.
* **Highlights & notes** lists everything in the book. Tap an entry to jump there, or **Export** it all as text, like "My Clippings".
* **Dictionary** (online):
  * meanings, examples, phonetics and 🔊 pronunciation, from the free dictionary
  * Wikipedia for names and places
* **Vocabulary**: saved words keep the sentence they came from.
* **Translate** into Nepali (or 11 other languages) with Google Translate.

## Reading comfort
* **Themes**: Light, Sepia, Dark and Black.
* Text size, font (Serif, Sans, Original, Mono), line spacing and margins.
* Remembers your place in every book, and shows progress and "Continue reading".
* **Read aloud** with the phone's voice.
* Search, the table of contents, and a slider to jump through the book.
* **"Open with Yogi Reader"** from Yogi Files, WhatsApp, Gmail or Downloads.
